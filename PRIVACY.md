# Privacy Policy: DoseDrop

Last updated: 11 October 2026

DoseDrop is a medication and water reminder app made by Dhiva Labs. This
policy covers the app on every platform it ships on (Windows, including the
Microsoft Store build, Linux and Android).

## Summary

DoseDrop keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, analytics,
telemetry, advertising or crash reporting, and it makes no network
connections. Your medications, schedules and history are never sent to Dhiva
Labs or anyone else.

## Data stored on your device

- **Your DoseDrop data**: the medications you enter (name, dosage, notes,
  colour, times, days), the history of doses you marked taken, skipped or
  missed, your water log, goal and reminder hours, and your settings. It is
  kept in one file, `dose_drop_data.json`, in the app's private data folder
  (on Windows, `%APPDATA%\Dhiva Labs\DoseDrop`, or the app's private package
  storage when installed from the Microsoft Store).
- **Alarm sound**: a copy of the app's alarm sound, `alarm.wav`, in the same
  folder, so the system sound player can play it.

Removing the app (or deleting that folder) deletes this data.

Medication information can be sensitive health information. DoseDrop does not
encrypt the data file; it is protected by your operating system account like
your other files.

## Notifications

Reminders are shown as local notifications through your operating system.
They are created on your device and do not pass through any Dhiva Labs
server. Notification text includes the medication name and dose, so it can
appear on your screen or lock screen depending on your system settings.

## Start at login and the tray

If you turn on "Start DoseDrop at login", the app registers itself with the
operating system's startup list (on the Microsoft Store build, Windows'
Startup apps list). You can turn this off in the app or in your system
settings.

## Backups

DoseDrop does not back up or sync your data. On Android, if you have turned on
your phone's system backup, Android may include the app's data in that backup
under your Google account; that is handled by Android, not by DoseDrop.

## Children

DoseDrop does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Medical disclaimer

DoseDrop is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice. Follow your
doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
